Where to apply

How the application works

No application is made on this page. Each card links to the provider's own portal, and the college verifies most applications before they move on.

₹
  1. Documents
  2. Portal
  3. College checks
  4. Bank account

Before you start: check that your bank account is linked to Aadhaar and mapped to the NPCI database, that your name and date of birth match across your Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets, Aadhaar and bank passbook, and that your income certificate is for the current financial year. These three points cause most rejected or unpaid applications.

Scholarship calendar

When does each one open?

Application windows follow the same months every year. Tap a month to see what you can apply for then, and plan your documents before it arrives. Dates are estimates, so confirm each one on the official portal.

Filled boxes show the months an application window is open.
